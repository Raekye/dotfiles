### Hotkeys
From <https://mpv.io/manual/stable/#keyboard-control>:

- <kbd>&leftarrow;</kbd> and <kbd>&rightarrow;</kbd>: seek backward/forward 5s.
- <kbd>shift</kbd> + <kbd>&leftarrow;</kbd> and <kbd>&rightarrow;</kbd>: seek backward/forward 1s.
- <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd>: decrease/increase playback by 10%.
- <kbd>backspace</kbd>: reset playback speed.
- <kbd>p</kbd> and <kbd>space</kbd>: toggle pause/resume.
- <kbd>m</kbd>: toggle mute/unmute.
