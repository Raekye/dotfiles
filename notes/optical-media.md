## CDs

- [cdrecord][cdrecord]/cdrtools: historically significant collection of projects for CD and DVD authoring.
- [cdrdao][cdrdao]: specializes in (audio) CDs.
- cdrskin/[libburn][libburn]: separate development; mostly compatible with cdrecord.
- wodim/[cdrkit]: fork of (~2006 May) cdrecord for Debian.

[libburn]: https://dev.lovelyhq.com/libburnia/web/wiki
[cdrecord]: https://codeberg.org/schilytools/schilytools
[cdrdao]: https://github.com/cdrdao/cdrdao
[cdrkit]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cdrkit
